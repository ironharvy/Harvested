# Chapter 1: The Hum Beneath Everything — Image Prompts

**Target model:** Nano Banana Pro

**Style direction for all prompts:** Muted industrial palette — greys, tarnished copper, dull amber, washed-out blues. Overcast lighting throughout. Painterly realism with a melancholy, cinematic atmosphere. No bright colours. Think Edward Hopper meets Andrei Tarkovsky — mundane spaces rendered with quiet dread.

---

### 6. B1 Containment Hall

**Placement:** Full-page illustration facing the paragraph beginning "The containment hall was cool and dry…"

```
A wide low-ceilinged underground hall lit by flat fluorescent strips. Rows 
upon rows of glass-and-copper cylinders racked floor to ceiling in steel 
frames. Each cylinder the size of a household water heater, etched with 
intricate suppression sigils that resemble decorative filigree. Copper 
contact leads running from each cell down into the floor. Narrow aisle 
between racks, barely wide enough for one person. The glass cylinders gleam 
under the cold light. Cool blue-grey palette with copper accents. A single 
figure in coveralls kneeling at a rack, reaching up to a calibration panel. 
Clinical, eerie, industrial cathedral atmosphere. Painterly realism, 
cinematic low angle, quiet horror.
```

### 7. Maren's Flat — Evening

## Production Notes

- **Image order follows the narrative:** The reader encounters each illustration at the moment its subject appears in the text, reinforcing the chapter's geographic progression: tram → station exterior → monitoring centre → containment hall → flat.
- **Full-page vs. half-page:** Locations central to the story's horror (Station Nine exterior, B1 containment hall) and the protagonist portrait get full-page treatment. Transitional or domestic spaces get half-page. Jorin's portrait is a small inset — he is present but secondary.
- **Colour consistency:** All prompts share the muted grey/copper/amber palette. The only warm light is the orange street lamp in the final illustration — a deliberate shift as Maren returns to her private space.
- **No text in images:** Prompts avoid requesting readable text (the "AUTHORISED PERSONNEL ONLY" sign is described as faded signage, not as legible text to render). The brass tram plaque is mentioned as an object, not as words to display.
- **Sigil design note:** The suppression sigils on the containment cells should look like intricate geometric filigree — beautiful enough to pass for decoration, which is the point the prose makes. They should not look overtly magical or runic.
